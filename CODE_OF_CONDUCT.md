# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want SensAI to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.
Contributors and maintainers commit to making participation a harassment-free experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are learning.
- Review the code, not the person: explain why a change is needed and suggest how to fix it.
- Accept constructive feedback and assume good intentions.
- Give credit for the work of others.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, harassment or discriminatory comments.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Sharing API keys, credentials or personal data in issues, pull requests or commits.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional or school setting.

## Scope

This code applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions and to any space where someone
represents the project.

## Reporting

Report any problem privately to the authors listed in [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md). Every report is read and kept
confidential. The authors may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code and may exclude
someone from the project.
